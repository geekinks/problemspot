# ProblemSpot Governance

## Principles

ProblemSpot is governed in the open. Decisions should prioritize community
safety, validated needs, maintainability, accessibility, and measurable impact.
Authority over the repository does not replace the lived experience of people
affected by a reported problem.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who reports issues, improves documentation, reviews designs, writes
code, tests solutions, or supports the community.

### Reviewers

Trusted contributors who regularly review work in a defined area. Reviewers may
recommend changes but cannot merge unless they also have maintainer access.

### Maintainers

People with repository write access who triage issues, review and merge pull
requests, manage releases, enforce community policies, and protect project
quality. Current ownership rules belong in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

### Community reviewers

Authorized people who review evidence and problem status. This product role is
separate from repository maintainership.

## Decision process

1. Small, reversible changes use normal issue and pull-request review.
2. Significant product, schema, security, privacy, or architecture changes
   require an issue or architecture decision record before implementation.
3. Maintainers seek rough consensus and document material objections.
4. If consensus is not possible, maintainers decide based on project principles,
   evidence, risk, and scope, then record the reason.
5. Security and safety emergencies may be handled privately and documented
   publicly after disclosure is safe.

## Becoming a reviewer or maintainer

Candidates should demonstrate sustained constructive participation, reliable
reviews, respect for project policies, and sound judgment around community data.
An existing maintainer proposes the role. Active maintainers approve it by
rough consensus and update repository permissions and `CODEOWNERS`.

## Inactive maintainers

A maintainer who is inactive for an extended period may move to emeritus status
after a good-faith attempt to contact them. Access should be removed promptly
when it creates a security risk or when the person requests removal.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers must disclose personal, employment, financial, or organizational
interests that could affect a decision and should recuse themselves when a
reasonable observer could question their impartiality.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes require a public issue, a review period, maintainer approval,
and a pull request explaining the reason and transition plan.
