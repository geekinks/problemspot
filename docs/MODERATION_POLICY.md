# Moderation Policy

## Purpose

Moderation protects people, evidence quality, and constructive collaboration.
It is distinct from problem validation: safe content can remain unverified, and
verified evidence can still require restricted access.

## Prohibited content and behaviour

- Harassment, threats, hate, intimidation, or targeted abuse.
- Doxxing, identity documents, private contact information, or unnecessary
  precise residential locations.
- Sexual exploitation material or content that endangers a child.
- Fabricated evidence, impersonation, spam, brigading, or vote manipulation.
- Unsupported accusations presented to provoke harm.
- Malware, credential theft, or instructions intended to compromise systems.
- Copyrighted or confidential material shared without authority.

## Moderation actions

Depending on severity and history, authorized moderators may:

- Request edits or additional context.
- Restrict evidence visibility.
- Add a warning or remove unsafe content.
- Lock discussion or limit participation.
- Merge duplicates while preserving attribution.
- Mark a report disputed, rejected, or closed with a reason.
- Suspend or ban an account.
- Escalate credible immediate risk through an appropriate lawful channel.

Moderators must use the least intrusive action that adequately protects people
and the platform. They must not silently rewrite reports or fabricate history.

## Process

1. Record the target, policy concern, actor, action, reason, and timestamp.
2. Preserve restricted evidence only when necessary for review or appeal.
3. Notify the affected user when safe and legally appropriate.
4. Separate public explanations from private evidence.
5. Apply consistent rules regardless of popularity or contributor status.

## Conflicts and appeals

Moderators must disclose conflicts and recuse themselves where practical. A
person affected by a moderation action may request review. A different
authorized moderator should decide the appeal when available, and the outcome
must be recorded.

## Emergencies and official complaints

ProblemSpot is not an emergency service, court, law-enforcement channel, or
government complaint system. The interface should direct urgent safety matters
to appropriate local services without promising intervention.

## Transparency

Publish aggregate moderation information when it can be done safely. Never
publish private evidence, reporter identities, or details that enable further
harm.
