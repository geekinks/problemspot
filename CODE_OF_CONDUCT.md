# ProblemSpot Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ProblemSpot welcomes contributors and community members of every background,
identity, experience level, and location. We are committed to a respectful,
safe, accessible, and constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for newcomers and people with lived experience of a problem.
- Ask before sharing another person's information, images, or story.
- Use inclusive language and accessible communication.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and move forward constructively.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or sexual attention.
- Doxxing or publishing private or identifying information.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Deliberate misinformation, fabricated evidence, spam, or vote manipulation.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Using community reports to target, shame, or endanger people.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, discussions, issue trackers, community
events, project communication, and public spaces where someone represents
ProblemSpot.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue when doing so could expose a
person or worsen harm. Use a private maintainer or community-reporting channel
published by the project. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may request a correction, edit or remove content, lock a thread,
issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately when
necessary, and protect the affected person from retaliation.

Anyone directly involved may request a review by a maintainer who did not make
the original decision, where one is available.

## Good-faith disagreements

Technical, product, and policy disagreement is welcome. Explain the user need,
evidence, trade-offs, and alternatives. Maintainers may close discussions that
repeat settled points without new evidence or become unsafe.
